01Stock

Two coasts

East and west.
Both in stock.

Domestic inventory changes the question from "when does the container land" to "when can you deliver".

01

A warehouse on each side of the country

Stock is held in Houston, Texas and Los Angeles, California. Order from the coast nearest the project and the inland leg is short, which matters most when a crew is already scheduled.

02

Dispatch within five business days of payment

Once payment clears, stock orders leave the warehouse within five business days. It is a commitment on dispatch, not on transit — the freight leg is quoted with the order so the delivery date is known before you commit.

03

Delivery to every US state

We arrange delivery nationwide, including jobsite delivery where access allows. Tell us the site and the receiving conditions and the freight quote covers the last leg rather than leaving it to you.

04

Tier 1 brands only

Every module we distribute comes from a BloombergNEF Tier 1 manufacturer. It is the bankability rating project financiers use, and it is the first question a lender asks about a module specification.

02Supply routes

Price or provenance

Imported, or
assembled here.

Most projects pick one route and stay with it. Some split the order so the domestic-content share covers only the tranche that needs it.

05

Imported modules, priced competitively

Container-sourced Tier 1 modules for projects where landed cost decides the bid. Same manufacturer tiering, same warranty structure, without the domestic-content premium.

06

US-assembled, with domestic content

Modules assembled in the United States with measurable domestic content, for projects claiming the domestic-content bonus or bidding into programmes that require it. Ask for the domestic content figure rather than assuming it clears a threshold.

07

Choosing between them is a project decision

The imported route wins on cost per watt. The domestic route wins on incentive eligibility and on lead time when the container market is tight. If the project qualifies for a domestic-content adder, model both before fixing the specification.

03Compliance

What the paperwork has to prove

Non-FEOC,
and traceable.

Compliance questions now arrive with the specification, not after the order. Documents should be able to answer them before anyone signs.

08

All products are non-FEOC

Every module we supply is outside the foreign-entity-of-concern definitions that govern US clean-energy incentives. Projects that must demonstrate this can do so from the supply itself rather than by substitution later.

09

Traceability documents on request

Supply-chain documentation is available with the order, so customs review, lender due diligence and incentive filing are answered from one pack. Ask which documents your programme requires and we will confirm what the shipment carries.

10

Warranty travels with the module

Tier 1 manufacturer warranty applies regardless of which route the module took. Product and performance terms are stated in the manufacturer's documentation for the specific model supplied, and confirmed before the order is placed.

04Ordering

From enquiry to truck

Five steps,
no container waiting.

The long lead times in this market come from ocean freight. Buying from stock removes that leg from the timeline.

01

Send the requirement

Power class, quantity, module format, and whether the project needs domestic content. That is enough to quote against stock.

02

Stock and freight confirmed

We confirm what is on hand at Houston or Los Angeles, and quote the freight leg to the delivery address alongside the module price.

03

Order placed and paid

The dispatch clock starts when payment clears. Documentation for the shipment is confirmed at this point.

04

Dispatch within five business days

Pallets leave the warehouse inside five business days of cleared payment, with tracking passed to the receiving contact.

05

Delivery and after-sale support

Delivery anywhere in the United States, with the same team handling shortages, damage claims and technical questions afterwards.

Something not covered? Ask our team ↗

How quickly can modules ship?+

Stock orders are dispatched within five business days of cleared payment. Transit time depends on the delivery address and is quoted with the order, so the expected delivery date is known before you commit.

Where do you ship from?+

From warehouses in Houston, Texas and Los Angeles, California. Orders are usually filled from the coast nearest the project, and delivery is arranged to any US state.

Are the modules BNEF Tier 1?+

Yes. Every module UniGlory Energy distributes comes from a BloombergNEF Tier 1 manufacturer, the bankability tiering project financiers use when reviewing a module specification.

Can you supply modules with domestic content?+

Yes. We supply both imported modules and US-assembled modules with domestic content. If a project is claiming a domestic-content bonus, tell us at enquiry so the domestic share is quoted correctly from the start.

Do you provide non-FEOC and traceability documentation?+

Yes. All products we supply are non-FEOC, and supply-chain traceability documents are available with the order for customs, lender and incentive filing purposes.
